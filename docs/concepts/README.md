## Consultation

Selected file: [consultation-v2.png](consultation-v2.png).

Initial generation prompt:

> Use case: ui-mockup. Create one high-fidelity full desktop web-app consultation screen for TILL DAWN, an AI skincare and makeup discovery platform for Singapore. This is a provisional design proposal, not a working app. Entire screen at landscape 1536x1024-like ratio, every edge and all controls visible. Elegant premium editorial style: cream #F8F0EC main background, dark chocolate #281F20 text, brown #70483F buttons, dusty pink #E4AFB9 accents, a very restrained silk-like rosy background at outer edges with small reflective chrome accent, generous whitespace. Cormorant-style serif headings with highly readable sans-serif interface type. Header: TILL DAWN wordmark on left, 'Skincare', 'Makeup', 'Consultation' navigation, subtle underline for consultation. No badge or eyebrow above headline. Main page heading 'Let’s find your next favourite.' Smaller copy 'Tell us what you love. We’ll help you explore the catalogue.' Slim left preferences column showing 'Your preferences', 'Makeup', 'Foundation', 'Natural finish', 'Up to S$40', 'Edit preferences', 'Start again'. Broad center chat area with an airy frosted cream panel but no nested bubbles around everything. One user message 'I’d like a lightweight foundation under S$40.' Assistant response 'Do you have a preferred finish? You can skip shade details for now.' Three subtle selectable controls: 'Natural', 'Dewy', 'Matte', selected Natural. Beneath, empty recommendation area labeled 'Your recommendations' and elegant text 'Verified catalogue products will appear here.' Do NOT invent any product names, brands, prices, recommendations, testimonials or matching metrics. Persistent composer at bottom with placeholder 'Ask about skincare or makeup…', small send arrow button, visible 'Skip questions and chat' text action and 'Clear chat' action. Small disclosure below composer: 'Cosmetic product discovery. Not medical advice.' Bottom footer links 'Privacy', 'Terms', 'Affiliate disclosure', 'Contact'. Practical accessible layout all text and controls clearly readable, minimum 16px body feel, strong contrast, no avatar, no voice microphone, no shopping cart, no fake product information, no floating decorative panels, no hero tagline badge, no excessive rounded boxes. Code-native text and controls intended for later Next.js/React implementation; original decorative background imagery must be separable. One complete primary screen only, not a collage or browser device frame.
